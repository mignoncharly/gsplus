9. Your rights and how to exercise them
In accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data in Cameroon, you may, subject to the conditions and limits in applicable regulations:

request correction, updating or, where the conditions are met, deletion;
request restriction of processing or object to processing under the conditions provided by law;
request data portability where that right applies;
withdraw consent for the future without affecting processing already lawfully carried out;
lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.
Send a written request to info@gsplus.vip stating its purpose and information identifying the data concerned. Proportionate proof of identity or further details may be requested to prevent fraudulent disclosure or deletion.

The scope of a right is assessed in light of the data’s nature, the basis for processing, legal obligations, third-party rights and the Studio’s need to establish, exercise or defend its rights. A request may be limited, adapted or deferred as permitted by law to protect the rights of others, confidentiality, security, trade secrets, intellectual property, anti-abuse methods or evidential needs.

The right to deletion is not absolute. Data may be retained where necessary to perform or prove a contract, for accounting or tax purposes, to process a payment or refund, prevent fraud, maintain security, comply with an obligation, protect third parties or defend the Studio. Deleting visible data does not necessarily cause immediate disappearance from backups, logs, evidence or restricted-access archives.

Withdrawal of image-rights authorisation is handled separately from requests to delete other personal data. It takes effect only for the future and does not affect uses previously made by the Studio in accordance with the original authorisation. It does not require recall or destruction of materials already produced or distributed, does not cancel completed campaigns and does not prevent retention of non-public copies needed as evidence. New uses cease after a technically reasonable period and sufficiently precise identification of the relevant content, subject to legal obligations, backups and third-party rights.
